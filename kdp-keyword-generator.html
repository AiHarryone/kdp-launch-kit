Tips for Getting More From Your KDP Keywords

Use All 7 Slots

Amazon lets you use up to 7. Leaving any empty means leaving search traffic on the table.

Mix Broad & Specific

Pair big terms ("bedtime story for kids") with specific ones ("unicorn book for children") to catch both audiences.

No Commas

Commas split a slot into two keywords and waste it. Spaces are fine — Amazon matches the phrase.

Think Like a Shopper

Use the words a buyer would type, not industry jargon. Plain phrases beat clever ones.
