Why Journals Are a KDP Staple

Journals are among the most reliable low-content products on Amazon: steady demand, evergreen topics, and buyers who often come back for a second design. The winning move is a specific niche plus a clear audience.

Journal Niches That Sell

  • Gratitude — for women, kids, teens, self-care
  • Wellness / habit — sleep, water, exercise trackers
  • Travel — trip memories, packing lists, travel logs
  • Food — recipes, meal planning, wine tasting
  • Hobby — reading, gardening, fishing, sewing
