The Short Answer

Publish both — ebook for reach and impulse buys, paperback for the print market. Most successful KDP books are available in both; each feeds the other and the setup cost is mostly one-time.

Ebook (Kindle)

  • 60% royalty at $2.99–$9.99 — no print cost.
  • Instant delivery — readers buy and read in seconds; impulse buys are huge.
  • KDP Select — exclusivity unlocks Kindle Unlimited reads (extra income per page).
  • Lower price point — $2.99–$4.99 is the norm for most indie fiction.

Paperback

  • 60% minus print cost — typically $1–$2.50 per book at $9.99.
  • Gift & print market — many buyers (and gift-givers) prefer a physical book.
  • Higher price point — $7.99–$12.99 is normal.
  • Free ISBN — KDP provides one at no cost.

When Both Makes Sense

Publish the ebook + paperback as the default pair. The ebook is the discovery engine (cheap, instant), and the paperback captures readers who insist on print. Add hardcover only if your category shows premium hardcover sales. Each format has its own correctly-sized files — use the free title & keyword generator to prep all of it quickly.

FAQ

Which format sells more? Ebooks usually sell more copies; paperbacks add a significant print audience.

Should I publish both? Yes — most successful KDP titles are in both formats.

Do I need separate files? Yes — each format needs its own correctly-sized interior and cover.

What royalty do I earn? Ebooks 60% at $2.99–$9.99; paperbacks 60% minus print cost.
