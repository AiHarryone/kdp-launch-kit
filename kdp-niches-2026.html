What Makes a Niche Profitable

A good KDP niche has steady demand, not too much competition, and room for a fresh angle — a specific audience, a better format, or a clearer title. The niches below have all three.

The Strongest Categories

  • Puzzle books — word search, sudoku, crosswords, mazes, dot-to-dot (huge, evergreen).
  • Coloring books — mandala, animal, Christmas, adult stress-relief.
  • Journals — gratitude, reading, prayer, fitness, habit tracking.
  • Planners — meal, budget, homeschool, weekly.
  • Children’s & educational — workbooks, picture books, activity books.
  • Hobby & lifestyle — cookbooks, sketchbooks, niche logs.

The Winning Angle

Within any category, the winners pair a clear audience with a specific format: “Gratitude Journal for Women”, “Large Print Word Search for Seniors”, “Homeschool Planner for Moms”. Generic titles lose; specific ones get found.

FAQ

What is the best KDP niche for beginners? Low-content (journals/planners) and puzzle books — fastest to make, steady demand.

Are coloring books still profitable? Yes — it’s saturated at the top, but specific themes and audiences still win.

How do I know a niche is profitable? Steady demand (ranked books with reviews) plus beatable competition (weak top-5).
