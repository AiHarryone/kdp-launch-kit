KDP DRM Guide: What the PDF/EPUB Download Change Means

What Actually Changed

Starting January 20, 2026, verified buyers of DRM-free Kindle e-books published through KDP can download the book as EPUB and PDF from Amazon’s “Manage Your Content and Devices” page. That means readers can move the file to a non-Kindle e-reader, app or PDF reader. Royalty rates and payment structure are unchanged — this change is about formats, not money.

New Books vs Existing Books

  • DRM-free books published after Dec 9, 2025 — EPUB/PDF downloads are enabled automatically. You do nothing.
  • DRM-free books published before Dec 9, 2025 — downloads are NOT enabled by default. You must opt in per book from your KDP Bookshelf.
  • Books with DRM enabled — no EPUB/PDF downloads. Readers use Kindle apps and devices only.
  • Kindle Unlimited borrows — not eligible, even for DRM-free books. Only verified purchasers can download.
  • Re-applying DRM later — stops future downloads, but readers who already downloaded keep their files.

How to Change DRM on a Book

  • Log in to the KDP Bookshelf and click the “…” menu next to the book.
  • Choose “Edit eBook Content” and scroll to the Digital Rights Management section.
  • Pick “Yes, apply Digital Rights Management” (keep Kindle-only) or “No, do not apply…” (allow EPUB/PDF downloads).
  • If going DRM-free, confirm the box: “I understand that by not applying DRM, customers who buy and have already bought this book will be able to download it as a PDF or EPUB file.”
  • Save and publish. Changes take 24–72 hours to go live. There is no bulk setting — update each book individually.

Should You Enable DRM?

DRM blocks piracy only a little — determined readers can already convert Kindle files with free tools. The real trade-off is reach vs control: DRM-free lets readers read on any device and is friendlier to buyers; DRM keeps downloads restricted to Kindle apps. Many indie authors are staying DRM-free because the change mainly reduces friction for legitimate readers. Decide per book, and you can change your mind anytime.

FAQ

Is the PDF/EPUB download automatic for my book? Only if it is DRM-free and published after December 9, 2025. Older DRM-free books need a one-time opt-in per book.

Can I still use DRM? Yes. You control DRM per book and can toggle it anytime from Edit eBook Content.

Do royalties change? No — royalty rates and payment structure are identical with or without DRM.

Can Kindle Unlimited borrowers download PDF/EPUB? No. Only verified purchasers can download; KU borrows are not eligible.

Is there a bulk DRM setting? No — you update DRM individually for each book in your KDP Bookshelf.
