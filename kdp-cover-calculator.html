KDP Cover Requirements

Kindle ebook: 1600 × 2560 px ideal (1.6:1). Min 1000px longest side. JPG or TIFF, RGB, max 50 MB.

Paperback/hardcover: 300 DPI, native resolution, 0.125" bleed, CMYK preferred.

Spine width formula: page count × paper thickness. White 0.002252"/page, cream 0.0025"/page.

Safe margin: keep text 0.5" from trim (paperback) or 0.625" (hardcover).

FAQ

Why is my Kindle cover rejected? Most common: too small (under 1000px), square 1:1 ratio, or wrong format. Use 1600 × 2560.

Do I need bleed on a Kindle cover? No — bleed is only for print. Kindle is front-cover-only, exact pixels.

Is this calculator free? Yes — completely free, no signup, runs in your browser. Always confirm with the official KDP Cover Calculator before ordering a print proof.
