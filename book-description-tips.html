The 30-Second Rule

Shoppers decide in seconds whether to keep reading. Your description’s first line must name who it’s for and the outcome, or they’re gone.

The Conversion Checklist

  • Hook in line 1 — “Looking for a screen-free activity book that keeps kids learning?”
  • Benefits, not features — what the reader gets, not what’s inside
  • Scannable — short lines, bullets, short paragraphs
  • Audience-specific — name the age range or reader type
  • Clear CTA — end with “Click Buy Now to get instant access.”

Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wall of text — no one reads a paragraph-heavy description
  • Features over benefits — “80 pages” is a feature; “keeps kids busy and learning” is a benefit
  • No audience — if you don’t say who it’s for, shoppers assume it’s not for them
  • No call to action — end with a direction, or you lose the click

FAQ
