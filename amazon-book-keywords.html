How Amazon Matches Your Keywords

When someone types “unicorn coloring book for kids” into Amazon, Amazon scans titles and keyword slots for matching phrases. The 7 slots you fill when publishing are prime real estate — they catch the searches your title doesn’t cover.

The 7-Slot Strategy

  1. Slot for the core topic — the plainest phrase, e.g. “coloring book”
  2. Slot for the audience — “for kids ages 4-8”
  3. Slot for the format — “workbook”, “activity book”
  4. Slot for a benefit — “screen-free”, “easy to read”
  5. Slot for a related term — “bedtime stories” if you cover that
  6. Slot for a season/occasion — “gift for kids”, “Christmas”
  7. Slot for a different angle — a synonym or alternate phrase
