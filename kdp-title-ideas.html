How the Best KDP Titles Are Built

Strong KDP titles do three jobs at once: they state the topic, signal the audience, and include a searchable phrase. “Number Tracing Workbook for Kids Ages 4-8” beats “Fun with Numbers” because a shopper can tell instantly what it is — and Amazon can match it to search queries.

Title Patterns That Rank

  • [Topic] + [Format] + [Audience] — the safe, high-converting structure
  • [Topic] Made Simple / for Beginners — strong for non-fiction
  • The Little [X] Who... — warm angle for children’s picture books
  • [Topic]: A Practical Guide — authority angle for how-to books

Real Examples

Non-fiction: "intermittent fasting"
Intermittent Fasting for Beginners Intermittent Fasting Made Simple The Intermittent Fasting Handbook
Fiction: "shadow"
Shadows of the Fallen Beyond the Shadow Gate The Shadow Chronicles
