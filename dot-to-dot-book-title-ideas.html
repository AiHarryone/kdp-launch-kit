Why Dot-to-Dot Titles Matter on Amazon

Dot-to-dot (connect-the-dots) books serve two audiences: kids learning numbers and adults doing relaxing, large-print puzzles. Titles should name the audience and often the theme — Christmas dot-to-dot is a big seasonal seller.

Dot-to-Dot Title Formulas That Rank

  • Dot-to-Dot for Kids Ages [X]-[Y] — the search-friendly classic
  • [Theme] Dot-to-Dot Book — “Christmas Dot-to-Dot”, “Animals”
  • Connect the Dots for [Audience] — “for Adults”
  • Large Print Dot-to-Dot — the senior / relaxation angle
  • [N] Dot-to-Dot Puzzles — volume signal

Titles That Undersell Your Book

  • No audience — dot-to-dot for kids and for adults are different markets; pick one.
  • Missing theme — Christmas, animals and vehicles sell far better than generic.
  • No number range — “counting 1-20” helps parents pick the right level.
