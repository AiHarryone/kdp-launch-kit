KDP Keyword Rules

7 slots, 50 characters each. Every slot is a chance to be found on Amazon search.

No commas. Commas split a slot and waste characters. Use spaces only.

Spaces count. Each space counts toward the 50-character limit.

Don't repeat. Don't reuse words already in your title, subtitle or category — it wastes discoverability.

FAQ

How many characters is a KDP keyword? Up to 50 per slot, 7 slots total.

Is this checker free? Yes — completely free, no signup, runs in your browser.

Can I save my keywords? They're saved in your browser's local storage automatically — close the tab and come back later.
