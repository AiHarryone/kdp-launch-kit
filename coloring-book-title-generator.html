What Makes a Coloring Book Title Sell

Coloring books live or die on search. A title that states the theme (unicorn), the format (coloring book) and the audience (for kids ages 4-8) gets found by the right shoppers — and passes the Amazon search test.

This generator creates titles that hit all three, tailored to whatever theme you type.
