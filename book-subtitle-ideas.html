Why Your Subtitle Matters

Your subtitle is the second sentence Amazon shows next to your title. It’s prime real estate for who it’s for and what they get — the two things that turn a browse into a click.

The Subtitle Formula

A strong subtitle is usually: “A [format] for [audience]” or “[outcome] — [how]”. Examples:

  • A gentle bedtime story for ages 3-8 — children’s picture book
  • A screen-free activity book for kids ages 3-6 — activity book
  • A practical guide for beginners and busy learners — non-fiction
  • A captivating novel that pulls readers in from the first page — fiction
