How KDP Royalty Works

Kindle ebooks: $2.99–$9.99 = 70% minus ~$0.15/MB delivery fee. Outside that range = 35%, no delivery fee.

Paperbacks: 60% at $9.99+ list price, 50% below, minus printing cost (page count + ink).

Hardcovers: 60% minus higher case-binding printing cost. Minimum 75 pages.

Expanded Distribution: Non-Amazon retailers pay 40% on print — always check your plan.

FAQ

Is the calculator free? Yes — completely free, no signup, no limits. It runs entirely in your browser.

Why is my paperback royalty so low? Printing cost eats into it. A 250-page black & white paperback at $14.99 nets about $4.99. Keep page counts lean to protect margin.

What's a good ebook price? $2.99–$9.99 gets you the 70% plan. Below $2.99 you drop to 35%.

Do delivery fees matter? Yes — $0.15/MB. A 10 MB image-heavy book loses $1.50 per sale. Compress images.
