The Two-Format Strategy

Children’s books on KDP do best published in both paperback and Kindle (ebook). The paperback is the gift/print market; the ebook lets parents try on a screen and often converts into the paperback sale. Upload the interior once, format it twice.

Step 1 — Trim Size & Layout

  • 8.5" × 8.5" is the most common picture-book size; 8" × 10" and 8.25" × 8.25" also work.
  • 24–40 pages is typical for picture books (KDP minimum is 24).
  • Fixed-layout for Kindle — children’s picture books need fixed layout so text stays with art.
  • Mind the spine — thick books and full-bleed spreads need bleed enabled.

Step 2 — Interior & Cover

  • Interior — one PDF with all pages, art reaching the safe area (keep text 0.25" from trim).
  • Cover — 2:3 ratio, 300 DPI, using KDP’s cover template for the spine.
  • Ebook images — 300 DPI JPGs or PDF; verify each page renders in the previewer.
  • Test both — KDP’s previewer shows print and Kindle; check spreads don’t split text.

Step 3 — Title, Keywords & Pricing

The title should state the character and hook (“The Little Unicorn Who Dreamed Big”); keywords cover the audience (“bedtime story”, “ages 3-8”).
