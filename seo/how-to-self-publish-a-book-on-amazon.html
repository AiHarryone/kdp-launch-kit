The Big Picture

Self-publishing on Amazon via KDP is free: you upload your manuscript and cover, KDP prints on demand (paperback/hardcover) or delivers as an ebook, and you earn a royalty on every sale. No gatekeepers, no upfront fees, worldwide distribution.

Step 1 — Prepare Your Manuscript

  • Write or design your content — a novel, a journal, a coloring book, a workbook.
  • Format to your trim size — export the interior as one PDF at the size you’ll choose.
  • Mind the page count — paperback minimum is 24 pages; hardcover 75.
  • Check the bleed guide if art reaches the page edge.

Step 2 — Create Your Cover

Use the cover dimensions guide and KDP’s Cover Calculator: a 2:3 front, spine included, 300 DPI. A clear, on-niche cover is often the difference between a click and a scroll-past.

Step 3 — Set Up Your KDP Title

  1. Create a free Amazon KDP account.
  2. Choose Kindle ebook and/or paperback; enter title, description and all 7 keyword slots.
  3. Upload your interior and cover files; run the previewer to check for errors.
  4. Set your price (see the pricing guide) and publish.

Step 4 — After Publishing

  • Order a proof — a physical copy is the best QA for print books.
  • Gather reviews — launch promos and ask readers.
  • Publish more — a line of books compounds far better than a single title.
  • Generate the listing in 30 seconds — title, description, keywords — with the free Launch Kit generator.

FAQ

Does KDP cost money? No — uploading and publishing is free; you only pay for author copies.

How long does publishing take? Ebooks go live in ~24 hours; paperbacks in a few days plus proofing.

Do I need an ISBN? No — KDP gives free ISBNs for paperbacks.

What do I need to start? A finished manuscript/design and a cover — everything else is free.
