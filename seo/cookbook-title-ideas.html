Why Cookbook Titles Matter on Amazon

Cookbook buyers search by diet (keto, vegan, Mediterranean), audience (beginners, busy families, college students) and meal (air fryer, slow cooker, 30-minute). A title that names the diet and the reader converts best.

Cookbook Title Formulas That Rank

  • [Diet] Cookbook for [Audience] — “Keto Cookbook for Beginners”
  • The Complete [Diet] Cookbook — authority angle
  • [Appliance] Cookbook — “Air Fryer Cookbook for Busy Families”
  • [N]-Minute Meals — “30-Minute Healthy Meals”
  • Easy [Diet] Recipes — “Easy Vegan Recipes for Beginners”

Titles That Undersell Your Book

  • No diet keyword — keto, vegan, Mediterranean are the search words buyers type.
  • No audience — “for beginners” and “for busy families” capture different buyers.
  • No recipe count — “200 easy recipes” sets expectations and converts.
