Picture Books Are Different

Picture books are visual: the layout matters more than text. That means fixed-layout for the ebook, careful trim-size choice, and full-bleed spreads handled with bleed enabled. Getting the mechanics right is most of the job.

Step 1 — Choose Size & Layout

  • 8.5" × 8.5" is the most common picture-book size (8" × 10", 8.25" × 8.25" also work).
  • 24–40 pages is typical; the paperback minimum is 24.
  • Fixed-layout Kindle — required so text stays with art (set this when creating the ebook).
  • Enable bleed if spreads reach the page edge (see the bleed guide).

Step 2 — Interior & Cover

  • Interior PDF — one file with all pages at trim size, art in the safe area (text 0.25" from trim).
  • Cover — 2:3 ratio, 300 DPI, spine included via KDP’s Cover Calculator.
  • Ebook images — 300 DPI JPGs or PDF; check every page in the previewer.
  • Test spreads — make sure the gutter doesn’t hide text on facing pages.

Step 3 — Title, Keywords & Pricing

Title with the character and hook (“The Little Unicorn Who Dreamed Big”); keywords cover the audience (“bedtime story”, “ages 3-8”). Generate title + subtitle in 30 seconds with the free children’s book title generator, price the paperback around $7.99–$12.99 and the ebook $2.99–$4.99.

How many pages? 24–40 is typical; the minimum is 24.
