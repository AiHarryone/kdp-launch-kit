Why Christmas Coloring Titles Matter on Amazon

Christmas coloring books are a seasonal bestseller — bought from late summer to December for kids, adults and as stocking stuffers. The winning title names the season, the audience and a festive character (Santa, reindeer, snowman).

Christmas Coloring Title Formulas That Rank

  • Christmas Coloring Book for [Audience] — “for Kids”, “for Adults”
  • [Character] Coloring Book for [Age] — “Santa Coloring Book for Toddlers”
  • Merry Christmas Coloring Pages — the classic generic that still works
  • Christmas Coloring Book with [Extra] — “with Funny Quotes”, “with Snowmen”
  • [N] Christmas Coloring Pages — “100 Festive Pages”

Titles That Undersell Your Book

  • No season word — “Christmas” or “Holiday” must be in the title for the search.
  • Wrong audience — kids’ and adults’ Christmas books are separate markets.
  • Publishing late — list by late summer; the season peaks early.
