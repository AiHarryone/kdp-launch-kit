Why Crossword Titles Matter on Amazon

Crossword buyers search by difficulty (easy, medium, hard), by print size (large print is a big keyword for adults and seniors) and by theme. Clear titles win because the shopper already knows exactly what they want.

Crossword Title Formulas That Rank

  • Easy Crossword Puzzles for [Audience] — “for Adults”
  • Large Print Crossword Book — the senior bestseller angle
  • [Theme] Crossword Puzzles — “Movie Quotes”, “Bible Crosswords”
  • Crossword Puzzle Book for [Level] — “for Beginners”
  • [N] Crossword Puzzles — volume signal that converts

Titles That Undersell Your Book

  • Too hard/unclear level — state easy/medium/hard.
  • Generic “Crossword Book” — add an audience or theme.
  • Forgetting solutions — most puzzle buyers expect answers included.
