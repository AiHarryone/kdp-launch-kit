The pricing sweet spot that keeps your royalty high and your book competitive.

The Short Version

For ebooks, price between $2.99 and $9.99 to earn the 60% royalty — the default choice for most indie authors. For paperbacks, price so that 60% minus print cost still leaves a real margin; most sell in the $5.99–$12.99 range. Below $2.99 or above $9.99 for ebooks drops you to 35%.

Ebook Pricing

  • $2.99–$9.99 — the 60% royalty band; $2.99–$4.99 is common for fiction and short reads.
  • $0.99–$2.99 — a promotional/launch price; you earn 35% but move copies and reviews.
  • Above $9.99 — rare for indie; only for premium non-fiction with real authority.
  • Price for the genre — check the top sellers in your niche and mirror their price band.

Paperback & Hardcover Pricing

Print royalty = 60% × price − print cost. A 200-page black-and-white paperback costs roughly $3.50–$5 to print, so a $9.99 book earns about $1–$2.50. Color interiors and hardcovers cost more to print — price those higher to keep a margin.

FAQ

What is the best price for a KDP ebook? $2.99–$9.99 — that band earns the 60% royalty.

How is paperback royalty calculated? 60% of the list price minus the printing cost.

Should I launch at $0.99? It can work to move copies and gather reviews, then raise to the 60% band.

Where can I see exact numbers? Use the free KDP royalty calculator, or check the pricing step in KDP.
