KDP Royalties — How Much Amazon Actually Pays

The 60%/35% ebook rates and how paperback royalty really works.

The Short Version

Ebooks: 60% royalty when priced between $2.99 and $9.99 (and enrolled in KDP Select), 35% outside that range or for most other cases. Paperbacks & hardcovers: 60% royalty minus the printing cost. So price your ebook in the 60% band, and price paperbacks knowing the print cost eats into royalty.

Ebook Royalty (the 60% / 35% split)

  • 60% — $2.99–$9.99 list price, for books in KDP Select (most indie books).
  • 35% — below $2.99, above $9.99, or not in KDP Select.
  • Delivery fee — on the 35% tier, a small delivery fee applies per MB (not on the 60% tier).
  • Regional price matching — royalties differ by marketplace; KDP shows per-market numbers.

Paperback & Hardcover Royalty

Print royalty = 60% × list price − printing cost. Printing cost depends on trim size, page count, paper type (white or cream), and whether the interior is B&W or color — color costs more. A thicker, bigger, or color book earns less per copy unless you price it up. KDP shows the exact print cost before you publish.

FAQ

What royalty does KDP pay on ebooks? 60% at $2.99–$9.99 in KDP Select; 35% otherwise.

How is paperback royalty calculated? 60% of list price minus printing cost.

Does print cost vary? Yes — trim size, page count and color interior all change the print cost.

Where can I see exact numbers? KDP shows print cost and royalty in the pricing step before publishing.
