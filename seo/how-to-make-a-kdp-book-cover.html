How to Make a KDP Book Cover

The 2:3 rule, free tools, and the details that make covers convert.

The Core Specs

  • 2:3 ratio — front cover height is 1.5 × width (8.5" × 8.5" book → 8.5" × 11" front).
  • Spine included — use KDP’s Cover Calculator template so the spine and back are right.
  • Bleed — add .125" around full-bleed art (see the bleed guide).
  • 300 DPI — export high-res; KDP rejects blurry covers.

Free Software That Works

  • Canva — free templates sized for KDP; drag-and-drop.
  • GIMP — free and powerful for full control.
  • Adobe Express — free tier with templates.
  • Google Slides/Docs — export as PDF at the right size in a pinch.

What Makes a Cover Convert

  • Readable at thumbnail size — most buyers see a ~200px thumbnail; test yours small.
  • On-niche design — mirror the visual style of the top sellers in your category.
  • Clear title contrast — the title must pop from the background.
  • Text away from the spine — keep the title centered on the front panel.
  • Preview in KDP — the previewer shows exactly what buyers see.

FAQ

What size is a KDP book cover? Front is 2:3; the full cover (with spine and back) comes from KDP’s Cover Calculator.

Do I need 300 DPI? Yes — low-resolution covers get rejected or look soft.

Can I use free software? Yes — Canva, GIMP and Adobe Express all work well for KDP covers.

Where do I get exact dimensions? KDP’s Cover Calculator generates them from trim size, pages and paper type.
