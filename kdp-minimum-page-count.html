KDP Minimum Page Count — The Rules and What Actually Feels Right

The KDP minimums, and the page counts that make a book feel real.

The Official Minimums

  • Paperback — minimum 24 pages.
  • Hardcover — minimum 75 pages.
  • Kindle ebook — effectively any length; short reads and long novels both work.
  • Check at upload — KDP may update limits; the setup screen always shows the current minimum.

What Actually Feels Right

  • Coloring books — 40–110 pages reads as a real book.
  • Journals & planners — 100–150 pages feels substantial.
  • Novels — 250–400 pages is typical.
  • Picture books — 24–40 pages is the sweet spot.
  • Low-content (trackers/logs) — 50–100 pages works.

Why It Matters

Minimums exist because KDP prints on real paper. Hitting the minimum is enough to publish, but page count also drives print cost (more pages = more expensive = lower royalty) and perceived value (a 30-page coloring book vs a 90-page one). Price accordingly — see the royalty guide.

FAQ

What is the KDP paperback minimum? 24 pages.

What is the KDP hardcover minimum? 75 pages.

Do ebooks have a minimum? Effectively no — any reasonable length works for Kindle.

Does page count affect royalty? Yes — more pages raise the print cost and lower the per-copy royalty.
